North Fourth Street

Latin American coffee, painted on the wall

Latin American · Organic Coffee

Mayan Winds Coffee Emporium is a small shop at 2144 N 4th St in Flagstaff. The sign over the door says coffee. The window says the rest in two languages: Kakaw and cacao, café and coffee, miel and honey.

Inside, a hand-painted mural in orange, with a red border, runs above the espresso bar. The figures are in the same family as the seated figure on the shop’s logo. Guatemalan textiles cover a table by the counter. Bagged coffee, honey, cacao, and mugs share the shelves with a pastry case, and a framed aspen landscape hangs over the booths.

Drinks on the board are built on a double espresso shot, unless a star says otherwise. French press, drip, and cold brew are the exceptions, along with a single espresso. The Mexican latte and the Mexican mocha are the house names on that board.

This page describes what is in the room. It is a concept preview, not an official history of the shop.
